The Problem · Why it matters

Tree cover looks like a park amenity. It functions like a utility.

When a block has mature trees, leaves do three measurable jobs at once: cast shade, transpire water (cooling air), and trap or reduce pollutants. When a block has none, residents pay the difference — in temperature, energy bills, and health.

Equity lens — visible on every page

The canopy follows the money — then magnifies the gap.

Across U.S. cities studied by American Forests and the USDA Forest Service, a ~$10k increase in median income correlates with roughly 2–3 percentage points more canopy in that tract. The mechanism is historical: redlining, highway siting, lot size, and decades of uneven planting budgets. The consequence is present-day: leafier tracts run cooler and cleaner.

Source: Sample synthesis — American Forests Tree Equity Score (2021), McDonald et al. (2021), and local canopy assessments. Full citations on The Data page.

Why it matters — plain language

Shade → lower temps → lower bills → better health.

❄️

Cooler streets

A block with 30% canopy can run 8–12°F cooler at the surface than a nearby 10% block. Air temp drops 2–5°F. That changes whether a school run or a delivery shift is safe in July.

Source: Ziter et al. (2019), Schwaab et al. (2021) — sample-marked synthesis.

💡

Lower energy & costs

Shade on west and south façades cuts cooling energy 15–35%. On exposed blocks, AC costs spike and brownouts hit harder — an equity cost.

Source: USDA Forest Service i-Tree synthesis — illustrative values.

🫁

Health & learning

Heat illness ER visits are 2–3× higher in the hottest tracts. Trees also filter PM2.5 (7–24% local reduction) and buffer stress — attendance and test scores track green access.

Source: Hoffman et al. (2020), Nowak et al. (2018).

What inequality looks like on foot

Two walks to school, same city — different canopies.

Laurel Heights · 38% canopy

Mature oaks every 7 m, light-colored sidewalks, a park within 300 m. Measured 31.2°C at 2pm in July. Parent survey: “We walk.”

AQI 42 · Good

Westfield Flats · 7% canopy

One sapling per block, dark asphalt, freight road 200 m away. Measured 40.8°C at 2pm in July. Parent survey: “We drive or skip.”

AQI 118 · Unhealthy for sensitive groups
